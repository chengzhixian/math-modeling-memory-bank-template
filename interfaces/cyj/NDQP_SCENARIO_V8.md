# CYJ Q2/Q3 conditional predictor v8

`cyj.ndqp.scenario.v8` is a new, versioned conditional model. The historical v7 code, fixtures and published output remain unchanged. The formal v8 baseline accepts `N_params_B`, `D_tokens_B`, a supported 17-domain `p`, 13-target unit weights, `p_policy`, and `quality_mapping_policy`; it **does not accept** a user-supplied B7 `Q_score`.

The baseline equation is

\[
L_{v8}(N,D,p;w)=\{E_{B1}+A_{B1}N^{-\alpha_{B1}}+B_{B1}D^{-\beta_{B1}}+[1-g(Q_A(p))][G_0+G_N\ln N+G_D\ln(D/100)]\}\exp[r_w(p)].
\]

The five classic coefficients come exactly from pinned `outputs/cyj/classic/classic_fit.json` (B1); the three quality-extension coefficients are fitted only from B7 with those five fixed. `outputs/cyj/q2_v8/b7_backbone_comparison.csv` reports train error, held-level and nested-CV error, complexity and the historical B7 joint-eight-parameter comparator. B7 is semi-synthetic, and the full B7 fit includes N/D rows outside B1 support; the formal predictor therefore restricts N to `[0.070542,11.965825]` billion parameters, D to `[10,299.893]` billion tokens, and proxy quality to `[0.1,1]`.

For `quality_mapping_policy=direct_and_near` (baseline), Q1's six mapped domains contribute their A1 score to `Q_A(p)=sum(p_i Q_Ai)/sum(p_i)`; `direct` uses only the three direct domains. Unmapped Q1 scores remain null. Zero mapped coverage is rejected, and both mapped coverage and unmapped mass are returned. The observed A1-score minimum/maximum under the selected mapping set normalize Q_A linearly into `[0.1,1]`; the Q1 reference mixture defines the anchor. `quality_bridge_scale=0.5,1,1.5` compresses, preserves or expands the difference from this reference, with clipping to B7 support. This is a chosen monotone cross-source proxy, **not** an empirical equality or fitted mapping between Q1 quality and B7's native `Q_score`.

The factor `exp(r_w(p))` consumes the frozen CHM Q1 v2 interaction surface and its own reference Loss denominators. At p_ref, `r_w=0`, so v8 Loss equals its B1-plus-quality base evaluated at the reference quality proxy. The quality proxy and interaction surface both depend on p; their overlapping content cannot be separated from these attachments. Returned p gradients therefore include both paths. Ambient p derivatives must be combined as feasible zero-sum directions within the Q1 A4 convex hull. `domain_pair_substitution.csv` and `domain_pair_interaction.csv` do this for all 136 domain pairs at the mean observed recipe, with hull-LP feasible epsilon limits and a model-based, noncausal interpretation.

The baseline method is `ConditionalV8.predict_baseline_v8(N,D,p,weights,*,p_policy,quality_mapping_policy)`. The sensitivity method is `evaluate(...)` with explicit `quality_mode=q1_quality_bridge_sensitivity` or `native_QB_sensitivity`, optional `quality_bridge_scale`, mixture `lambda`/`eta`, and `exp_bridge` or `linear_bridge`. The public JSON request parser in `src/cyj/ndqp_scenarios_v8.py` rejects unknown baseline fields, nonfinite values, unsupported compositions, invalid quality coverage, and nonpositive linear factors. The frozen `interfaces/cyj/fixtures_v8/` request/expected pairs provide independent consumption examples. The Q3 owner must consume an exact released v8 version before claiming owner verification; this interface alone does not complete Q3 optimization.

`quality_scale_local_tradeoff.csv` separates the local compensating derivative `dN/dQ=-L_Q/L_N` (negative when both improvements reduce Loss) from the finite *old-Q equivalent scale increase* and the finite *new-Q constant-L compensation*. B3's normalized trajectory check is quantitative but derived from B1-style Pythia curves, not independent external validation; B2 is semi-synthetic stress. B4/B5 comparisons report only within-family directional agreement and support status because common absolute Loss coordinates are unverified. B9 remains metadata, B10 estimated stress. No A/B paired calibration or combined prediction interval is claimed.
