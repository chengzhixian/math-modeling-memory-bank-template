# Q2 bridge identifiability

For every observed B row, p is unrecorded; for every A mixture row, B-native Loss and Q_B are unrecorded. Both lambda=0 and lambda=1 give the same B predictions when p=p_ref, and the A-native contrasts do not depend on lambda. They are observationally indistinguishable in the available data, but at any nonzero A relative contrast their conditional B predictions and potentially Q3 allocations differ. Thus lambda is not identified. The A scale groups also change training conditions and lack complete D; eta is a predeclared sensitivity axis, not a pure N estimate.

The A target reference denominators are positive fitted Ridge predictions at p_ref. They are not a B calibration, and target weights are decision utilities. The Q_A to Q_B map remains unidentified. B7 bootstrap or OOF intervals cannot cover this bridge uncertainty.
